Legal Notice, Disclaimer, Privacy & Credits.

Telugu States by CSRGO is an independent, non-commercial 3D artistic community simulation created strictly for entertainment purposes.

1 · Entertainment & Fictional Simulation

No Real Land, Property, or Financial Value

  • Purely virtual fiction: All houses, village lanes, courtyards, crops, and “1 acre” plots in Telugu States are stylized 3D digital illustrations inside an entertainment experience. Nothing on this website or Android app grants, implies, records, or represents real-world land ownership, cadastral survey boundaries, tenancy, or property rights.
  • Virtual community resources only: Food, Currency, and Gold counters are fictional world-building numbers used tounlock new visual features and community milestones. They are not money, legal tender, securities, tokens, cryptocurrency, sweepstakes, lottery entries, or redeemable credits, and cannot be bought, sold, traded, or cashed out.
  • Free & voluntary: Exploring the map and 3D landscape is completely free with no purchases, subscriptions, fees, or financial transactions.
2 · Neutrality & Real-World Names

Non-Political, Non-Religious & Caste-Neutral

  • Zero affiliation or representation: This project does not represent, promote, favour, or affiliate with any religion, religious institution, caste, community group, political party, political figure, movement, or government authority.
  • Geographic familiarity only: Real names of Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, their 61 districts, rivers, hills, and landmarks come from open public geographic datasets and are used solely for geographic familiarity and artistic orientation.
  • Generated villages & plain names: Village streets, building layouts, gardens, weather, and water levels are procedurally generated fiction — not real villages, real homes, or real private property. Plain first names or nicknames on wooden signboards do not identify real residences.
3 · Platform Transparency & Anti-Spam

Independent Project & Fair Community Conduct

  • Not affiliated with Instagram or Meta: Telugu States by CSRGO is an independent creator project and is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Instagram, Meta Platforms, Inc., or any government department.
  • Anti-spam policy: Community resource milestones (1 like = 1 food, 1 comment = 1 currency, 1 share = 1 gold) reflect manually reviewed aggregate engagement on official community posts for shared world-building only. Automated bots, repetitive comments, artificial engagement, harassment, or spam are strictly prohibited and excluded.
4 · Privacy & Zero-PII Architecture

What Happens on Your Device

  • No raw usernames stored: When you enter an Instagram username to locate your virtual home, your browser or Android app normalizes it in memory and computes an irreversible 256-round SHA-256 resident ID (h + 24 hex characters). The raw username is immediately discarded and is never stored in project files, URLs, servers, or logs.
  • Local-only storage: Only the anonymous one-way resident ID and display preferences (language, camera, graphics quality) are saved locally in your browser’s localStorage or Android app preferences so you do not need to re-enter your username.
  • No tracking or intrusive permissions: No passwords, emails, phone numbers, contacts, GPS location, microphone, camera, advertising IDs, or third-party analytics cookies are collected.
  • Immediate removal / opt-out: Anyone who wishes to rename or remove a virtual house record may message @telugustates.csrgo at any time for prompt removal.
