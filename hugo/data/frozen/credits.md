# Field atlas · data credits

This is a community simulation, not a cadastral survey or a claim to land. Only the two states are retained. No *real* streets, settlements, building footprints or home addresses were collected. Streets in the atlas are a **generated village layout, not a map of real roads**. Buildings, gardens, planted tree forms and procedural details are stylized artwork. The `village/` component and frontage masks are derived from the frozen district, slope, water and mining channels described below, not from OSM roads.
