# How the shared treasury works

No designated reel or verified snapshot has been supplied. All three balances are zero, no observation time is claimed, and the share count is **unavailable**, not a verified zero. Nothing runs against a social account. No credentials or write interface are shipped to a browser.

An authorized operator designates eligible reel IDs in `data/economy/config.json`, compares cumulative metrics in an authorized export or the owner's Insights, keeps the supporting evidence privately, and supplies a reviewed import. Every record needs `reel`, offset-bearing `reportedAt`, `source` (`official-export` or `manual-review`), `reviewer`, `evidence` reference, `status`, and `metrics` containing `likes`, `comments`, `shares`. Use `null` for unavailable amounts. The script validates provenance fields; it cannot verify the truth of a screenshot. Pending records earn nothing. Do not add invented records to the production files. `python scripts/update_economy.py --check` is a reconciliation, never a mint. To commit an authorized import on a POSIX build machine: `python scripts/update_economy.py --apply --at ISO_TIMESTAMP_WITH_OFFSET --import-file VERIFIED.json`. Inspect the audit, Git diff and state before committing or publishing.

Each positive new like/comment/verified share yields one food/currency/gold respectively. 1,000→1,250 likes credits 250, not 1,250. A later decrease is recorded at zero credit; the high-water mark prevents a rebound beneath the old peak from earning twice. Backdated conflicting observations are rejected for review. The append-only hash chain records source, cumulative observations, signed transactions and balance after each event. Its integrity is verifiable from trusted Git history; a hash by itself is not an external signature.

Reviewed residential occupancy has an effective date (`from`, inclusive, `until`, exclusive) in `occupancy.json`. Enrol from the next local day after publication; keep a closed interval after retirement. Close **completed** Asia/Kolkata civil days before crediting fresh imports. Each day uses min(available, occupied × rate); unmet demand is reported without debt or deletion. Previously closed rosters cannot be silently changed. There is no fuel, crop or engagement fallback. More than 10 days is Healthy, 5–10 Stable, 2–<5 Low, <2 Shortage and zero food with occupied homes Crisis. No houses means no food consumption. No shortage is described as an actual drought.

Gold is held unless a human approves an `exchange` order. One gold can be exchanged for ten currency, both sides in one atomic journal. A `project` order includes the village district and an eligible registered site. Both kinds need a unique `id`, `approved:true`, `approvedBy` and `evidence`. Use `--orders FILE`. Repeated orders cannot spend twice. Balances and sufficient inhabitants/need/suitable site are checked before spending. No global upgrades, no random schools, no off-site bridges. Currency and gold have no redeemable value.

**Recovery:** commit the reviewed input and ledger together. A process lock guards concurrent writers and a write-ahead journal completes interrupted multi-file replacements. Preserve the journal until recovery and replay succeed. If an operator discovers wrong verified facts, freeze publication, compare the last trusted Git revision and supporting evidence; do not edit old balances in place. Back up state before policy migrations. Builds read published state, not private credentials.

## Sustainability check (hypothetical, not Instagram data)

Over 30 days at one unit per occupied home per day, demand for **0 / 10 / 100 / 1,000 / 10,000** homes is **0 / 300 / 3,000 / 30,000 / 300,000** food. At a hypothetical 75 newly verified likes daily, supply is 2,250; 10 homes are sustainable, 100 lack 750, 1,000 lack 27,750 and 10,000 lack 297,750. At 300/day, 100 homes are sustainable, but 1,000 lack 21,000. At zero verified likes, all occupied sizes eventually run short. These are sensitivity scenarios, not projections of campaign performance. Any proposed rate change or agricultural supplement needs owner approval.
