# Walk in Telugu States

Start at the regional map. Tap **Explore in 3D**, or choose a landscape in **Explore Beautiful Places**. The world is drawn from local files; you don't need to sign in, turn on location, or use mobile data. A virtual acre is not a claim to real land.

- **Move and look:** put one thumb anywhere on the left half and drag. The stick appears under that thumb; push to its edge to sprint. Drag the other half to look. Pinch with **two fingers on the look half** to change camera distance. The camera shortens its boom before hitting a tree, house, rail, bank or water surface. In open ground the follow distance is about 3.5–6 m. You can look up without losing the traveller.
- **Actions:** tap Jump for a short hop, or hold for a higher one. Hold Sprint, or double-tap it to lock; double-tap again to unlock. Movement, looking, Jump and Sprint have independent contacts. An occupied plot has a gate, not an invisible way through its wall. In shallow water you slow down. In deep water you swim slowly; you cannot jump off the water as though it were solid ground.
- **Other controls:** WASD or arrows move; mouse drag looks, wheel zooms, Shift runs, Space jumps. On browsers supporting a standard-mapped controller, left stick moves, right stick looks, A jumps and the left shoulder or left-stick press runs. The settings can mirror touch controls, invert look, change sensitivity and optionally recenter behind a moving character. Controller mapping was tested with injected standard input, not a physical controller.
- **The map and menu:** the arrow in the upper left goes back to the map. The chip at top center shows the district and simulated weather. Tap it or swipe down on it for the district/state, coordinates, elevation, plot code, acre size and credits. There are separate labels for **World time** (including when a lighting preset is in use) and **Local time** (the device clock). A fixed lighting preset is not today's time or forecast. The default shared clock completes one visual day in four real hours.
- **Houses:** tap a nearby visible @name. The sheet opens from the bottom. Open the Instagram profile, share the virtual house, walk to it, or close the sheet. Nothing is sent just by opening it. Demo homes are available only in the explicit developer view; no search result is invented.
- **Quiet screen:** controls fade after about four seconds without input. Brief messages announce entering an area, a change of footing or the start/end of rain. Tap the × on the three-step tutorial to dismiss it. Reopen it in **Menu → Controls & camera**. This choice and the control preferences stay on the device.
- **Photo:** **Menu → Controls & camera → Photo mode** hides play controls. Drag to look, pinch or use two fingers to move; on desktop use WASD/scroll. The Camera button adjusts height, hour and weather for the picture. Save a PNG; on Android you choose the file. Exit Photo to return to the same place and normal settings.
- **Battery and vibration:** Battery saver selects the Low rendering path, caps draws around 30 per second, uses matte water and suppresses costly effects and jump/landing vibration. A cap is not a promise of 30 FPS. Vibration is optional, respects system settings and requires device support.

## How to return a usable device reading

1. Install/update **7.0.0 (70001)**. Don't uninstall or clear app data. On the Bapatla walk (15.991240 N, 80.260022 E), use **Menu → Measure this walk → Start measurement**. Keep walking during the 12-second reading; a paused menu or standing still is not a movement measurement.
2. Do this in portrait and landscape on Auto. Repeat after about ten minutes of walking, then try Battery saver separately. Check simultaneous thumbs/actions and maximum upward pitch, the screen edges, a resident pin, Back, Save picture, and optional vibration and audio. Note any heat warning or stutter. Don't guess a temperature.
3. In the walking measurement panel, tap **Share readings** to open Android's chooser; paste the JSON into the conversation or attach it as a file. Add whether the run was cold or warm and what you saw. The JSON includes build, model/Android/WebView from the launcher, viewport, safe insets, draw tier, distance, and moving-only FPS/p95/worst frame. Zero motion reports no moving FPS. No background upload or phone account access is used.

The browser test can reproduce thumb events and read rendered pixels. It cannot certify an actual handset's cutout, chooser, speakers, haptic motor, heat, or sustained frame pacing.

## Integrated public edition

Start on the map and tap **Explore in 3D**. Rotate the phone at any time. Put one thumb down to move, drag the other side to look, and hold Sprint or tap Jump with another finger. The menu can mirror these controls, lower motion, replay the tutorial or switch to Battery Saver. The return arrow reopens the map; Explore resumes the same walk. In **Find your house**, enter a handle, @name or Instagram profile link. If no record has been published, use the join instructions instead. Tap a place card to walk without a house. The details chip shows separate world and local clocks. In Menu choose shared/Live/manual time, season and weather. Use **Sound and music → Tap to start sound**; the five sliders stay on the device. Photo mode removes the HUD. Use **View the community treasury** on the home page for verified balances and land measures. With no public residents yet, look at the labelled developer-only before/after photos rather than expecting a funded village on the public map.
