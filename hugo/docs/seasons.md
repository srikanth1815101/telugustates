# The simulated year

Twenty-eight real days advance one shared year. Its twelve phases follow winter, summer, southwest monsoon and retreating monsoon. State, location, coast distance and rainfall evidence shape the front probabilities. A manual season is a viewing choice, not a change to shared state. Crop stages derive from the year and fixed field seed. Elevation, coastlines and allocated acres do not change with the season. This is not a forecast, a crop calendar for farmers, or an estimate of this year's rain.
