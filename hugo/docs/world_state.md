# What persists, and what doesn't

**Permanent, in Git:** one resident Markdown file per house, the plot lock, boundaries, elevation, land-cover and water freeze, the reviewed community roster, dated occupancy, the engagement history, ledger and approved work. Source dates and attribution are in the frozen credits. A release derives static search/plot shards and frontage only from those inputs. Preparing the site does not import an observation, spend currency, consume food or rerun the one-time migration.

**Shared, computed:** time and season are offsets from the published epoch, and weather is seeded by region. They continue while the app is closed. Correcting the device clock may change the simulated sky. **Personal only:** language, sound gains, controls, visual overrides and optional walk timings. Neither local storage nor a URL parameter can publish a resident or credit a treasury. Saving a photo is a user action. `?dev=1` opens the historical fixture viewer; ordinary routes do not count those records. The public projection isn't access control for a private database: never put private resident or Instagram data in the static tree.

**Streaming:** a map visit downloads the regional outline/relief and small public indexes, not WebGL. On entry into the world, the near terrain window loads only its intersecting tiles; the old window keeps supporting the traveller until the new collision mesh is ready. Old tile/plot and GPU batches are evicted on swap. Crop row geometry simplifies with distance. The shipped terrain uses a stronger lossless DEFLATE encoder; its original uncompressed elevation and masks are identical. `scripts/pack_transport.py` checks every original SHA, every decoded tile and records both transport and source checksums. It operates on a publication, not on the freeze. Production is plain static files: parallel workers are local to the page; no server-side loop, permanent service, runtime credentials or compulsory network requests.
