# Walking trails, not roads

24 branches at 12 mapped places; 18.64 km. Max source grade 0.245. Exploration paths use a least-cost search over neighboring DEM cells. It penalizes slope and climb, excludes mines, mapped water, steep cells and assigned plots, and checks diagonal corners. One branch heads toward a screened high point, another toward a clearing. They are simulated routes, not hiking advice. No invented bridges or rivers. Coarse terrain cannot certify individual footfalls. The motor still checks rendered ground.

Occupied homes keep their own short 0.9 m entrance, a 1.4 m gate connection and a 1.85–2.30 m village path. Only branches needed by published homes appear. Anchored curves in a reservation never move a home. Natural paths and local utility upgrades are distinct; exploration paths never get poles.
