# Sound in the landscape

Open Sound and music in the walking menu and tap to start. Master changes everything; Music, Effects, Nature and Weather can be set separately. Mute and the music switch are saved on this device. A hidden or paused scene suspends its audio. No microphone, download or music service is used.

The score is six original, lightly overlapping synthesized instrumental phrases: morning, day, evening, night, rain and exploration. Stronger rain ducks the score. Footfalls use short filtered noise and the animation contact signal. Wind, water, weather, animal calls and occasional settlement sounds are stylized, not documentary recordings. Pan and filtering depend on context. The combined mixer is capped at 24 active or scheduled sources. No encoded tracks or third-party audio bytes are required. Real speaker balance still needs headphone and phone listening.
