# Nature & Motion · Android test 5.1.0-nature.1

This is an offline exploratory build, not a release or a survey of current land use. The seven user-supplied pictures directed silhouette, colour, clothing and water treatment; no pixels, character meshes or commercial asset packs were imported from them.

**Walk it.** Enter the reservoir shallows from the phone launcher. Push the joystick forward from the dry, muddy bank, through reeds and pebbles, until the body floats; push back to walk out again. Run toggles pace. In the field menu, select a traveller, or choose **Look at traveller** to see the face and outfit. Dragging the camera around an idle traveller does not rotate the body. Mouse/keyboard: drag, WASD, Shift, Space. Phone shortcuts open Medium / Winter / Day. The live IST, seasonal, quality, atlas survey and map controls remain available.

**What is built.** The landscape renderer contains no villages, streets, floor pads, houses, domestic herds, crops, scarecrows, carts, shrines, boats or planted rows. Place lookup still uses the 300 original resident handles. The only structures are two-rail timber fences on actual assigned acre edges; a 4.6 m opening is left on the frontage, even on shared edges. Travel starts inside the assigned acre, not at a phantom door. Both the 3D survey and the Map pin point to the acre, not a stored house footprint. Old house metadata is preserved solely for continuity; no residents were moved and no one-time migration was run.

**The water.** Filtered wet classifications, the original mapped polylines (sampled as smooth curves) and the ocean polygons determine the shoreline. The height of a wet vertex is estimated from wet DEM observations rather than from the adjacent high bank. Water depth along the approach and channel widths when the source omits them are *illustrative*. The wet mask is not a collision wall. Terrain, feet, buoyancy and water use one local grid of sampled triangle heights. The water material mixes depth tint, a translucent shelf, animated bank wash, sky colour, flow-oriented wave normals and six recent moving-body ripples. This is a shallow-water visual and a buoyancy controller, not a hydrodynamics solver or measured bathymetry.

**The walker.** Two separately built low-poly people use a 16-joint skin and metre-scale proportions. Jackets, open plackets, cuffs, hands, eyes, hair, boots, a cap or scarf and packs are modelled geometry. Feet have alternating world-space support anchors, two-link knee solutions, lifted swings and opposing arm action. The motor integrates in substeps (at most 1/72 s), with acceleration/braking, gravity, grade-dependent pace, wading drag, river drift and a damped floating height. A prone stroke and flutter kick take over in deeper water. This is a procedural gait, not motion capture or a full-body rigid-body simulator.

**The ground.** Four original tileable pigment channels represent thatch, sand, silt and stone. Material blending follows geography, soil/wetness, elevation and slope; physical blades, reeds and weathered boulders cover a small streaming neighbourhood underfoot. Timber, grass, skinned character, sand/mud and responsive water are present on Low as well as Medium/High. Distant relief is still actual terrain, not an invented mountain skyline.

**Source checks.** From the checkout: `python -S scripts/prepare_site.py --check`, `python -m pytest -q`, `node tests/nature_walk.mjs`, `node tests/ecology.mjs`, `node tests/water_grade.mjs`. `hugo --minify -s site -d /absolute/new/public` creates the canonical publication; use `Landscape-Android` from Workspace Files for the Android wrapper, installer-specific checks and signed-asset browser tests. Historical v5 scenery tests and screenshots in the earlier commit describe that earlier scene, not this one. Actual frame rate, touch feel, thermal load and WebView compatibility need a physical phone test.
