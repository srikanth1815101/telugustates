# Where to go

On the home page, choose a card under **Explore the landscape**, or expand **All 12 field stops**. In 3D, open the menu, choose a Field guide stop and select **Walk**, **Overlook** or **Panorama**. Append a route below to your own atlas URL. Use `/mid` or `/far` instead of `/ground` for the two upper cameras. These coordinates are the actual sampled cells, not invented regional labels.

**Go to your house on a phone.** Search your username or tap an individual house pin. From the two-state 3D view tap a district label, spread two fingers to descend, then tap the pin or **Visit @…**. You land at the house entry, not the middle of the acre. On the ground, spread two fingers to shorten the follow camera into first person; pinch inward to pull back. The joystick walks; drag empty terrain to look. Tap **Map**, then **3D** to resume the same view. Tapping a home pin instead deliberately goes to that home's doorstep, even if the URL already contains its username. With no WebGL 2 the paper map, search, plot and Markdown-derived home record still work. A temporary graphics loss offers a retry.

**Menu controls.** Season: Winter mist / Summer gold / Monsoon green. Light: Golden hour / Day / Dusk / Live IST. Relief exaggeration: 1.0×–3.0× (default 2.0×); the HUD always gives real elevation. Quality: Auto / Low / Medium / High. Nearby-life labels are optional. Settings are saved on this browser when storage is available.

**Handset performance check.** Open this atlas on the actual Android phone, not a desktop emulator. Set Medium, Monsoon green, Golden hour. Visit Village, Canopy trail, Reservoir shore and Escarpment walk, on the ground. Under the menu's **Measure this walk**, tap Start and walk forward using the joystick; stay in the foreground. Let Village and Forest run for 60 seconds, the other stops for at least 12. Copy the JSON results, phone model and Chrome version. No data is sent anywhere. Report a pause, graphics loss or slow scene rather than averaging a bad run away.
