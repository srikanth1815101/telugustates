# A quicker river, a moving bank

At **River bend**, stand still on the bank. Follow a leaf or the break in a reflection past the fixed boulder. Look down and along the bank: a low patch floods for a moment and then shows dark earth again. Do not turn on rain. In orbit, try a range of **42 metres**. The open reservoir and the coast are controls, not two other places to see the same current.

## What was measured in the region

- Johnson, Sivakumar & Rosenfeld (2017), [*Ecological flow requirement for fishes of Godavari river*, Current Science 113, 2187](https://www.currentscience.ac.in/Volumes/113/11/2187.pdf). Four pool/run transects below the then-proposed Polavaram dam; March–May 2012. Across 414 paired observations, water-column velocity ranged from **0 to 2.1 m/s**. The most frequent class was **0.9–1.2 m/s (33%)**. Readings were taken with a current meter at 40% of depth below the surface, not by tracking surface foam. The fast end was uncommon.
- Shemeera K. H. & Sravani Duvvuri, [*Water quality modeling of Krishna River reach using QUAL2Kw model*, Table 1](https://www.jetir.org/papers/JETIRA006019.pdf). A shallow substream below Prakasam barrage near Vijayawada, March 2018. At five stations the float-based velocities were **0.2091–0.2975 m/s**. A different reach and measurement method; not a universal speed for the Krishna, or a bank/core ratio.
- Horoshenkov et al. (2013), [*The pattern of surface waves in a shallow free surface flow*](https://doi.org/10.1002/jgrf.20117). In rough gravel-bed flume experiments, subsurface turbulence produced structured surface waves whose scales depended on the flow. This informs the **local, carried** ripple treatment; those experiments do not measure overtopping heights on an Indian riverbank.

## What the game chooses

The game's River bend is a virtual location in Sri Sathya Sai district, **not one of those survey transects**. Velocity is in metres per liquid second. Frozen vector tangents and longitudinal head gradients orient mapped reaches; unlabelled widths are not treated as survey data. A rising curve of gradient selects a faster active run, capped at 2.1 m/s. Shallow margins slow and turn along the fitted shore rather than driving water marks through dry land. A mapped inflow under standing head keeps a separate slower law; an open, unmapped pool has no downstream current. The visible parcel and the surface skin use the local current; the swimmer experiences only bounded partial drift. This is deliberately legible game tuning toward the fast end, not a conversion of river discharge into speed.

On the shallow running shelf, metre-scale moving pressure patches raise and depress the water by centimetres. Their phase travels downstream, and their size varies along the bed. Ground above a local crest stays dry. Recently covered soil darkens briefly. In the local views the signed water depth is clipped against a finer cut of the **same bed** that the boots sample; both use the same vertex displacements and elapsed liquid clock. The margin effect fades outside the local mesh; the regional overview remains cartography. No new texture, video or model is streamed into the game. The patches model neither a change in basin storage nor a real flood, measured bank microtopography, or a periodic sea wave.

Run `node tools/check-river-margin.mjs` (Hugo on PATH) for nine measured cross-bank lines at each of two moving reaches and a reservoir control. `node tools/check-littoral.mjs` checks the independent beach swash and current paths. The sibling [`Android/Checks/`](../Android/Checks/) folder contains timed images and signed-game measurements.
